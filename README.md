# Workforce Attrition & Retention Risk Dashboard

I built this Power BI dashboard to understand why employees leave a company and spot early warning signs before they do. I used the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset from Kaggle for this.

## What I did
I looked at attrition across departments, job roles, and how long people had been with the company. I also built a simple "risk flag" to identify employees who are more likely to leave, based on short tenure and overtime work.

## What I found
Employees with under 2 years at the company who were also working overtime had a 55% attrition rate — almost 4x higher than everyone else (14%). That's a clear early warning signal HR teams could actually act on, like checking in with new hires who are working overtime.

## Tools I used
- Power BI (building the dashboard and writing the DAX formulas)
- Power Query (cleaning the data)
- Dataset: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition (Kaggle)
